# Customer Discovery Interview Guide: Specialist CRM

**Goal:** Validate (or kill) the wedge from the [research brief](./dental-crm-research-brief.md): a referral-relationship CRM for endodontists, oral surgeons and periodontists, sitting on top of the practice-management system (PMS) they already use.

**Target:** 15–20 interviews over 4–6 weeks.

| Segment | # | Who to talk to |
|---|---|---|
| Endodontics | 4–5 | Owner doctor + referral coordinator / office manager |
| Oral surgery | 4–5 | Owner doctor + practice administrator or physician liaison |
| Periodontics | 3–4 | Owner doctor + office manager |
| DSO / multi-specialty group | 3–5 | Operations or growth leader responsible for specialty referrals |

**Format:** 30 minutes, recorded with consent. Ask about past behavior, not opinions about the future. Show the demo only in the last 5 minutes, and only after the discovery questions.

**Do not** collect or view any patient information during interviews.

---

## Screener (send before booking)

1. Practice type: endo / oral surgery / perio / multi-specialty / DSO
2. Number of locations and doctors
3. Approximate share of new patients that come from referrals: <25% / 25–50% / 50–75% / >75%
4. Practice-management software: Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental / OMSVision / WinOMS / TDO / PBS Endo / Denticon / Dolphin / other
5. Role: owner / associate / office manager / referral coordinator / ops leader
6. Patient-communication tools in use (Weave, NexHealth, RevenueWell, etc.)

**Qualify:** referral share ≥ 50% (or DSO with ≥ 1 in-house specialty), and the interviewee owns, or influences, software decisions.

---

## Interview script

### 1. Context (3 min)
- Walk me through your practice: locations, doctors, team, and who handles referrals.
- Roughly how many new referrals do you get in a typical week?

### 2. How referrals arrive and flow (7 min)
- Tell me about the last referral you received. How did it arrive (fax, phone, email, portal, patient walk-in)?
- What happens next, step by step, until the patient is treated?
- Where do referrals get stuck or fall through? How do you find out?
- How long does it typically take to get a referred patient scheduled?
- When was the last time a referred patient never booked? What happened?

### 3. Reporting back to referrers (5 min)
- What do you send the referring dentist, and when?
- How long does it take to produce? Who does it?
- Have you ever had a referring doctor complain about communication?

### 4. Managing referrer relationships (8 min) ← core hypothesis
- How do you know who your top referrers are? Show me, if you can.
- When did you last notice a referrer had stopped sending patients? How did you notice, and how long after it started?
- What did you do about it? Did it work?
- What outreach do you do (visits, lunches, CE, study clubs, gifts)? Who does it, and how is it tracked?
- If a top referrer dropped by half, how much revenue would that cost you a year?
- *(DSO)* How do you measure referrals that leave the network? What does that cost you?

### 5. Current tools and spend (4 min)
- What do you use today for any of this: PMS reports, spreadsheets, a CRM, a liaison, nothing?
- What do you pay for patient communication and marketing tools each month?
- What did you try before and stop using? Why?

### 6. Prioritization and willingness to pay (3 min)
- If you could fix one thing about how referrals work in your practice, what would it be?
- Imagine a tool that alerts you when a referrer starts drifting, tracks every referral to closure, and sends reports automatically. Where would that rank against your other priorities this year?
- What would it have to do to be worth $300–$500 per location per month? What would make it a no?

### 7. Demo and close (5 min, optional)
- Show the CRM demo: dashboard → a lapsed referrer → the referral pipeline → the referral portal.
- What's missing? What would you remove?
- Would you be willing to be a design partner? That means a free pilot in exchange for feedback, with a 30-minute check-in every two weeks.
- Who else should we talk to?

## Decision criteria (after 15+ interviews)

- **Proceed with this wedge** if all of the following hold:
  - ≥ 60% rank referral relationship tracking as a top-3 problem.
  - ≥ 40% say yes or maybe at $300+ per location.
  - ≥ 3 sign up as design partners.
- **Re-scope** if the pain is real but sits mostly in report-back or scheduling. That is a narrower, operations-focused product.
- **Pivot the wedge** if specialists say their PMS already covers this adequately. In that case, test the full-arch / high-ticket case funnel (Phase 2 in the brief) instead.
